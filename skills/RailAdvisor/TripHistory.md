## Summer 2024 — The Grand Loop

**Route:** Nottingham > London > Brussels > Cologne > Koblenz > Mannheim > Basel > Chur > Tirano (Bernina Express) > Lake Como (Lierna) > Milan > Florence > Pisa > Genoa > [Ferry] > Barcelona coast (Calafell, Castelldefels, Canet del Mar) > Perpignan > Paris (overnight train) > London > Nottingham

**Duration:** 32 days (Jul 25 - Aug 25)
**Budget:** GBP 11,618 total
**Pass:** 10-day Eurail Pass (GBP 1,006)
**Accommodation total:** GBP 6,753
**Daily food budget:** GBP 50/day

### Key Details
- **Bernina Express** was a highlight — Chur to Tirano with lunch break in Tirano
- **Genoa to Barcelona overnight ferry** (GNV, 23 hours, GBP 375)
- **Spain base-hopping** — three coastal locations south/west/north of Barcelona
- **Overnight train** Paris-bound via Perpignan (SNCF Intercites de Nuit)
- **Guests** ("J&S") joined for part of the Spain villa stay (Aug 7-14)
- Birthday celebrated during Canet del Mar stay (Aug 21)
- Train reservations via mix of Rail Europe, DB, Swiss Rail

### Accommodation Pattern
- Transit cities: 1 night hotels (London, Koblenz, Chur, Paris)
- Mid-stay: 2-3 night hotels (Lake Como, Florence, Genoa)
- Extended anchor: 8 nights villa + 2 nights Airbnb + 7 nights rental (Spain coast)

## Established Patterns for 2026 Planning

### Travel Style
- **Rail-first philosophy** — no flights for the family, trains and ferries only
- **Early morning departures** — consistently books 05:00-09:00 train starts
- **Loop routes** — always starts and ends at Nottingham via London/Eurostar
- **Eurostar is the gateway** — London to Brussels/Paris, always reserved early

### Budgeting
- **GBP 50/day food budget** — consistent across both years
- **GBP 1,000 activities budget** — consistent
- **Total budget range:** GBP 7,500-11,600 depending on duration
- **Per-night accommodation:** ranges from GBP 130 (Chur) to GBP 350 (Paris)
- **Tracks payment status meticulously** — cards, deposits, balances, cancellation dates

### Accommodation
- **Mix of types** — hotels for transit, villas/holiday parks for extended stays
- **Extended anchor stay** — both years had one location for 8-10 nights
- **Uses Booking.com primarily** — Airbnb for specific needs
- **Cash deposits** sometimes required for Spanish villas

### Route Preferences
- **Scenic trains** are a priority (Bernina Express 2024, Rhine route noted)
- **2-3 day city stops** for sightseeing, longer anchors for relaxation
- **Flexible with connections** — happy to take regional trains between hubs
- **Night trains used** — 2024 used Perpignan sleeper back to Paris
- **Ferry used** — 2024 Genoa-Barcelona overnight (happy with long crossings)

### Booking Behaviour
- **Rail Europe** for Eurostar and cross-border high-speed
- **DB** for German reservations (cheap, ~GBP 10-11 each)
- **OBB** for Austrian/international via Austria
- **Direct operator sites** for Italian trains
- **Eurail/Interrail pass** — used 10-day flexi pass in 2024
- **Books reservations well in advance** — spreadsheet tracks exact booking references

### Spain Experience (relevant for 2026)
- **Familiar with Rodalies/Cercanias** — bookmarked timetables, line maps
- **Barcelona coast well-known** — stayed at Calafell, Castelldefels, Canet del Mar
- **Renfe booking issues** — UK card rejection is a known problem
