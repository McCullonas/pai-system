## 1. Accommodation Types on Night Trains

### Sleepers (Proper Beds - Premium)

Private compartments with 1-3 stacked beds along a corridor car (10-12 compartments per car).

| Configuration | Description | Typical Supplement | Class Required |
|---------------|-------------|-------------------|----------------|
| **Single** | Bottom bed only, others folded away | ~EUR114 (EUR40-70 in Eastern Europe) | Varies (many now accept 2nd class) |
| **Double** | Bottom + middle beds, top folded | ~EUR74/person | Varies |
| **Triple/Tourist** | All three beds deployed | ~EUR54/person | 2nd class on all routes |

**What's included:**
- Proper beds with mattress, sheets, blankets/duvet
- Washbasin with toiletries provided
- Carpeted compartments that convert between sitting/sleeping mode
- Attendant service
- Breakfast on Nightjet and many EuroNight services
- Sparkling wine on Nightjet sleepers

**Deluxe sleepers** (Nightjet): Private shower and toilet in compartment. Available as single or double.

**Security:** Standard lock PLUS secondary deadbolt/chain that cannot be opened from outside, even with staff key. Nightjet Comfortline uses hotel-style card keys plus CCTV in corridors.

**Power:** Most older sleeper cars lack standard outlets (only shaver sockets). Nightjet Comfortline cars have laptop/mobile charging ports beneath berths. Always bring a power bank.

### Couchettes (Budget Bunks)

Padded bunks in shared compartments. Passengers arrange their own bedding (provided: rug/blanket, sheet, pillow).

| Type | Berths | Typical Supplement | Notes |
|------|--------|-------------------|-------|
| **6-berth** | 6 bunks (3 per side) | ~EUR34 | Most economical. Mixed gender normal. |
| **4-berth** | 4 bunks (2 per side) | ~EUR44 | More space. Only ~EUR10 more than 6-berth. Worth it. |
| **1st class couchette** | 4 berths | Same supplement | Slightly larger/plusher. France only. |

**Berth positions (6-berth):** Berths 1-2 bottom, 3-4 middle, 5-6 top.
**Gender:** Mixed in couchettes (you don't fully undress). Women can request ladies-only compartments on many routes.

**French Intercites de Nuit couchettes** include: pillow, sleeping bag, bottled water, amenities kit.

### Reclining Seats (Not Recommended)

Available but not advised for overnight travel. Same compartment-style seating, much less comfortable than lying flat.

**Seat61 verdict:** "Always book a couchette rather than just a seat, so you can sleep lying down in a safely-locked compartment."

### Key Advice on Accommodation

- "A 2nd class couchette is more comfortable (and more secure) than a 1st class seat"
- The EUR10 upgrade from 6-berth to 4-berth couchette is extremely worthwhile
- Don't use "1st class" and "2nd class" terminology for sleepers -- it's about occupancy level
- Berths are sold individually: 1 ticket = 1 person = 1 bed
- 2 people booking a double sleeper get the entire compartment to themselves
- After 35 years of travel, Seat61 reports zero security problems in sleepers

**2026 Warning:** Significant trackwork on these routes for much of 2026. Check details a few weeks in advance for altered times and possible rail replacement buses.

**Interrail reservation fees:** EUR10 (reclining seat) or EUR21.60 (couchette).

## 3. Interrail Pass - Complete Guide

### What It Is

Unlimited train travel on scheduled services of national train operators across 33 European countries. NOT special "Interrail trains" -- you ride regular trains.

### Eligibility

European citizens or legal residents of participating countries. Non-Europeans use Eurail (now functionally identical).

### Pass Types & 2026 Pricing (2nd class adult)

**Continuous passes:**
| Duration | Price |
|----------|-------|
| 15 days | EUR476 |
| 22 days | EUR586 |
| 1 month | EUR696 |
| 3 months | EUR956 |

**Flexi passes:**
| Travel Days | Validity | Price |
|-------------|----------|-------|
| 4 days | 1 month | EUR283 |
| 10 days | 2 months | EUR447 |

**Discounts:**
- Youth (under 28): Available on all pass types
- Children under 12: FREE (max 2 per adult)
- Seniors (60+): Available

### Countries Covered (33)

Austria, Belgium, Bosnia, Bulgaria, Croatia, Czech Republic, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Great Britain, Greece, Hungary, Ireland, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Montenegro, Netherlands, North Macedonia, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Serbia, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Turkey.

**NOT covered:** Russia, Ukraine, Albania. Also NOT covered: private operators like Italo (Italy) or certain premium services.

### Home Country Rule

Interrail does NOT provide unlimited domestic travel. You get 2 outbound/inbound days -- one journey leaving your country, one returning. These can involve multiple trains but must be a single logical journey to/from a border.

For UK residents, Eurostar counts as domestic travel.

### Night Train Rule (Important!)

For flexi passes: overnight trains use only ONE travel day -- the departure date -- provided you don't change trains after midnight.

### Mobile vs Hard-Copy

| Feature | Mobile | Hard-Copy |
|---------|--------|-----------|
| Delivery | Instant | Posted (extra cost) |
| Activation | Any date within 11 months | Date fixed at purchase |
| Management | Rail Planner app | Manual travel diary |
| Validation | Needs internet every 3 days | Physical pass |
| Battery risk | Yes -- bring power bank | No |

### Pass vs Point-to-Point: When Pass Wins

- Crossing 4+ borders
- Many journeys planned
- Short-notice travel (when advance fares are expensive)
- Flexibility to change plans / reroute
- Pass loses on: fixed itinerary with advance booking possible, few journeys

**Example:** 5-day flexi pass = EUR318 youth = EUR64/day. Prague-Berlin advance = EUR18.99. But short-notice might be EUR95-167, making pass competitive.

### Reservation Zones

| Zone | Countries | Reservation | Cost |
|------|-----------|-------------|------|
| **Green (pass-friendly)** | Germany, Austria, Netherlands, Belgium, Switzerland, Ireland | Optional | EUR3-6 |
| **Yellow** | Central/Eastern Europe | Often required, cheap | EUR1-3 |
| **Red** | France, Italy, Spain, Portugal, Sweden | MANDATORY on intercity | EUR10-20+ |
| **Special** | Eurostar | Mandatory | EUR35-40 |

## 5. Booking Strategy & Tips

### When to Book

| Operator/Route | Booking Window |
|---------------|----------------|
| Eurostar | Up to 11 months ahead |
| SNCF TGV (France) | Up to 6 months ahead |
| Nightjet | Up to 6 months (Brussels route: 3-4 months) |
| European Sleeper | 6 months ahead |
| French Intercites de Nuit | Should be 4 months, often only 2 months |
| Spanish railways (Renfe) | 15 days to 9 months ahead (varies) |
| Italian railways | Loads data gradually |

**Timetable reset:** European railways reset schedules December 12th annually. French, Italian, Spanish operators load data only months ahead, so missing trains doesn't mean cancelled.

### Where to Book

| Platform | Coverage | Fees | Currency | Notes |
|----------|----------|------|----------|-------|
| **thetrainline.com** | Multi-operator, wide | Small fee | GBP/EUR/USD | Good seat selection |
| **raileurope.com** | Multi-operator, wide | Small fee | GBP/EUR/USD | Easy to use |
| **sncf-connect.com** | French railways | No fee | EUR | Best for France, seat selection, no onward Spain |
| **oebb.at** | Austrian/Nightjet | No fee | EUR | Most detailed Nightjet options |
| **renfe.com** | Spanish railways | No fee | EUR | Fiddly interface |
| **europeansleeper.eu** | European Sleeper | No fee | EUR | Direct only |
| **b-europe.com** | Belgium/Eurostar/Thalys | Varies | EUR | Passholder Eurostar bookings |

**No single website covers all European trains.** For multi-country journeys, you typically need multiple tickets from different operators.

### Splitting Tickets to Save Money

**Paris-Barcelona split at Perpignan:** Example showed Paris-Barcelona at EUR170, but Paris-Perpignan (EUR60) + Perpignan-Barcelona (EUR29) = EUR89 total (47% saving). Use "More options" feature to add Perpignan stopover.

### Key Booking Principles

1. Book as early as possible for cheapest advance fares
2. Break complex journeys into segments -- often reveals cheaper options
3. Check the destination country's operator -- sometimes cheaper than origin country
4. Most operators now issue e-tickets (print or phone)
5. Some still require collecting physical tickets at station machines
6. International payment cards work better on thetrainline.com and raileurope.com than national sites

## 6. Route: Paris to Barcelona

### Option A: Direct High-Speed TGV (Recommended)

**Operator:** SNCF, TGV Duplex double-deck trains
**Speed:** Up to 320 km/h (199 mph)
**Distance:** 1,073 km (667 miles)
**Journey time:** 6h39 - 6h50

**2026 Schedule:**

| Direction | Depart | Arrive | Stops |
|-----------|--------|--------|-------|
| Southbound | 07:42 Paris Gare de Lyon | 14:32 Barcelona Sants | Nimes, Montpellier, Perpignan, Figueres, Girona |
| Southbound | 14:42 Paris Gare de Lyon | 21:32 Barcelona Sants | Same |
| Northbound | 09:26 Barcelona Sants | 16:12 Paris Gare de Lyon | Same |
| Northbound | 16:24 Barcelona Sants | 23:18 Paris Gare de Lyon | Same |

**Pricing:**

| Category | 2nd Class | 1st Class |
|----------|-----------|-----------|
| Advance | From EUR39 | From EUR59 |
| Return advance | From EUR78 | From EUR118 |
| Full flexible | EUR259 | EUR279 |
| Child 4-11 | From EUR39 | From EUR59 |
| Child 0-3 | Free (no seat); EUR9 with seat | -- |

**Interrail:** Pass accepted. Mandatory reservation required (see France reservation fees).

**Train amenities:**
- Power sockets at every seat (European 230V 2-pin)
- Free WiFi ("Le WiFi" network)
- Cafe-bar (upper deck car 4) -- can order on phone to skip queue (lebar.sncf-connect.com)
- Upper and lower decks (upper recommended for views)
- 1st class: 2+1 seating. 2nd class: 2+2 seating
- Both classes "absolutely fine, no need to pay for 1st class"

**Luggage:** Up to 3 pieces max 85x55x35cm totaling 25kg + 1 hand item max 46x70cm under 30kg. Bikes free if semi-disassembled in zip bag under 120x90cm.

**Station tips:**
- Paris Gare de Lyon: No check-in. QR ticket gates close 2 minutes before departure. Platform posted ~20 mins before.
- Barcelona Sants: X-ray baggage screening required. Left luggage lockers available.
- Consider dining at Train Bleu restaurant inside Gare de Lyon.

**Money saver:** Split booking at Perpignan can save up to 47%.

### Option B: Sleeper via Latour de Carol (Mountain Route)

**Schedule:**
- Depart Paris Austerlitz 22:13
- Arrive Latour de Carol 10:04
- Connect 10:26 local train
- Arrive Barcelona Sants 13:46

**Frequency:** Fri & Sat all year, daily June-September.

**Pricing:**
- Paris-Latour: EUR20 (seat), EUR35 (2nd class couchette), EUR63 (1st class couchette)
- Latour-Barcelona local train: EUR12.35

**Highlights:** Crosses highest point on any standard-gauge European railway (1,562m). Castle at Foix visible. Latour de Carol is a unique triple-gauge station. "Wonderfully scenic" mountain views in final hour.

**Practical:** No cafe car. Eat before boarding, bring supplies. Bistro de la Gare at Latour station for breakfast between connections.

### Option C: Sleeper via Cerbere (Coastal Route)

**Schedule:**
- Depart Paris Austerlitz 20:08
- Arrive Perpignan 08:28 / Cerbere 09:16
- Option A: High-speed Perpignan 11:18 -> Barcelona 12:38
- Option B: Local coast train Cerbere 12:04 -> Barcelona 15:10

**Frequency:** Fri & Sun all year, daily in summer.

**Pricing:**
- Paris-Perpignan/Cerbere: EUR22 (seat), EUR29 (2nd class couchette), EUR60 (1st class couchette)
- Perpignan-Barcelona (AVE): EUR25-45
- Cerbere-Barcelona (local): EUR14

**Highlights:** Runs along Cote Vermeille through Port Vendres & Collioure with sea views. Local coastal train calls at Figueres (Dali museum) and Girona.

**2026 WARNING:** Significant trackwork on both sleeper routes for much of 2026. Check details a few weeks in advance.

### Option D: Renfe AVE S100 via Lyon/Montpellier

Barcelona connects to southern France via Renfe AVE high-speed trains.

**Schedule:**
- Barcelona departs 08:14, arrives Lyon 13:20
- Barcelona departs 16:31, arrives Lyon 19:23
- Also serves: Girona, Figueres, Perpignan, Narbonne, Montpellier, Nimes, Avignon, Marseille

**Pricing:** From EUR25 (2nd) or EUR45 (1st).

**Features:** 300 km/h, free WiFi ("PlayRenfe"), power sockets, cafe-bar, 1st class 2+1, 2nd class 2+2.

## 9. General European Train Travel Tips

### Station Navigation

**Paris station changes** (critical for connections):
- Gare du Nord (Eurostar) to Gare de l'Est: 7 min, 500m walk
- Gare du Nord to Gare de Lyon: Requires metro/taxi (allow 45+ min)
- Gare de Lyon to Gare d'Austerlitz: 10 min walk across Seine bridge

**Tip:** Avoid Paris station transfers when possible. Consider changing at Lille instead.

### Connection Times

- Through-tickets: Legally entitled to later service if connection missed
- Separate tickets: Delay risk is entirely on you -- allow 40+ minutes
- Platforms posted 15-30 minutes before departure (Germany/Austria)

### Luggage

- European trains are generous with luggage -- no airline-style limits on most services
- "There's always room for luggage in any class"
- Left luggage available at major stations
- Bikes usually require advance booking + small fee

### Food & Drink

- Long-distance trains have restaurant cars or bistro services (quality varies)
- Buy supplies at stations beforehand as backup
- "Always take provisions with you, including perhaps a bottle of wine" for sleeper trains
- Premium 1st class on some services includes at-seat catering

### Classes

- 2nd class is "genuinely comfortable" on modern European trains
- 1st class: wider seats, more legroom, fewer passengers, sometimes included food
- "No food or drink included in 1st class" on most trains -- it's just the space

### Timetables & Real-Time Info

- **int.bahn.de**: Europe's most comprehensive timetable
- **Rail Planner app**: Offline timetable for Interrail users
- Country-specific: viaggiatreno.it (Italy), renfe.com (Spain), sncf-connect.com (France)
- Punctuality tracking: zugfinder.net

### Documentation

- E-tickets accepted on most services (print or phone)
- Passports not typically needed within Schengen
- Eastern European borders may require brief passport checks on night trains

### WiFi & Power

- Major trains in France, Germany, Switzerland: Free WiFi
- Nightjet: WiFi + 220V sockets
- French TGV: Free WiFi ("Le WiFi")
- Spanish AVE: Free WiFi ("PlayRenfe")
- Regional/older services: May lack connectivity -- download offline maps
