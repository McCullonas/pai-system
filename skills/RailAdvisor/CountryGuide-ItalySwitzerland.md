# PART 1: ITALY

## 1.1 Train Operators

### Trenitalia (www.trenitalia.com)
- State-owned, dominates Italian rail network
- Operates all Frecciarossa, Frecciargento, Frecciabianca, Intercity, and regional services
- Also operates Frecciarossa Paris-Milan/Turin services (since 2021)

### NTV Italo (www.italotreno.it)
- Private competitor on high-speed routes
- Operates AGV trains on major corridors
- Competitive pricing, modern amenities
- NOT covered by Interrail/Eurail passes

### Trenord (www.trenord.it)
- Regional operator in Northern Italy (Lombardy)
- Operates Milan-Tirano regional service (connects with Bernina Express)
- NOT covered by Italia In Tour pass

### Circumvesuviana Railway (www.eavsrl.it)
- Independent operator: Naples-Pompeii-Sorrento corridor
- Every 30 minutes, no reservations needed
- Fares: ~EUR 3.20 to Pompeii (40 min), ~EUR 4.50 to Sorrento (55-65 min)
- Campania Express: 4 daily reserved air-conditioned services for EUR 15

---

## 1.2 Train Types

### High-Speed Trains

**Frecciarossa (Red Arrow) - up to 300 km/h**
- Flagship service on Turin-Milan-Bologna-Florence-Rome-Naples-Salerno corridor
- Models: Frecciarossa 1000 (since 2015), 500, 600, 700
- Classes: Standard (2+2), Business (2+1), Executive (10 reclining seats per car)
- Amenities: cafe-bar, power sockets at all seats, free WiFi
- Business class: complimentary coffee, juice, croissants (prosecco in PM)
- Executive class: at-seat food and prosecco on china, FrecciaClub lounge access
- Reservation: MANDATORY, included in ticket price

**Frecciargento (Silver Arrow) - up to 250 km/h**
- Tilting trains, increasingly rebranded as Frecciarossa
- Air-conditioned, cafe-bars, power access
- Being phased out / merged into Frecciarossa branding

**Frecciabianca (White Arrow)**
- Older tilting trains, now rare
- Primarily Milan-Genoa-Pisa-Rome route
- Refurbished equipment

### Intercity Trains
- Locomotive-hauled, 160-200 km/h
- Open-plan or compartment seating
- Most have vending machines rather than full catering
- Passengers may bring own food and beverages
- Reservation: MANDATORY, included in ticket

### Intercity Notte (ICN) - Overnight/Sleeper
- Connect major cities with Sicily
- Berths, couchettes, varying comfort levels
- Typical delays: 30-90 minutes on Sicily routes
- Trains go onto a ferry at Villa San Giovanni for Messina Strait crossing
- "Last remaining place in Europe where passenger trains go onto a ferry"

### Regional Trains (R and RV)
- Unassigned seating, no catering, fixed pricing
- No reservation needed or possible
- Modern double-deck "Rock" trains alongside older stock
- Unlimited availability - cannot sell out
- Bikes accepted for ~EUR 4 with bike ticket

## 1.4 Interrail/Eurail Pass Usage in Italy

### Coverage
- **Covered**: All Trenitalia services (Frecciarossa, Intercity, Regional)
- **NOT covered**: Italo/NTV trains
- **NOT covered**: Circumvesuviana Railway

### Reservation Supplements (with pass)
- Frecciarossa: EUR 13
- Intercity: EUR 3
- Regional trains: No reservation needed, ride free with pass
- Reservations: Online or at staffed counters only

### When Passes Are Worth It
- Multiple long-distance journeys
- Passengers under 28 (youth passes cheaper)
- Flexibility to hop on/off trains spontaneously
- For point-to-point, advance-purchase fares often cheaper

## 1.5 Practical Tips

### Luggage
- No baggage fees, weight limits, or check-in
- Self-carry onto trains
- Overhead racks, car-end racks, between-seat spaces
- Level access at all major termini (street to train, wheelchair friendly)
- Left-luggage facilities at all main stations

### Food & Drink
- High-speed: cafe-bars serving wine, beer, coffee, snacks, hot dishes
- Intercity: usually vending machines only
- Regional: no catering
- **"Feel free to bring your own food and drink, even a bottle of wine"**

### WiFi & Power
- Free WiFi on all Frecciarossa trains
- Power sockets at all seats on high-speed services
- Regional trains: varies

### Ticket Validation
- High-speed/Intercity: TICKETLESS - show booking reference on phone/printed
- Regional (from ticket offices/machines): MUST validate in green machines at platform entrance
- Regional (online purchase): No validation needed
- **Tap & Tap**: Contactless payment on selected routes (Florence-Pisa-Livorno; Venice-Verona)

### Real-Time Information
- www.viaggiatreno.it - live departures, arrivals, train tracking
- Railplanner app - offline timetables, shows reservation requirements

### Italian City Names (for booking)
- Roma = Rome
- Firenze = Florence
- Venezia = Venice
- Napoli = Naples
- Milano = Milan
- Torino = Turin
- Genova = Genoa

### Punctuality
- Regional trains: generally on schedule
- High-speed: average 10-20 minute delays
- Overnight sleepers to Sicily: 30-90 minute delays possible

### Pets
- Small dogs/cats in containers (under 70x30x50cm): FREE
- Larger dogs (muzzled, on lead): 50% adult 2nd class fare
- Guide dogs: free
- Not allowed in Executive/Premium or catering cars

### Bicycles
- Regional/InterRegional: accepted with ~EUR 4 bike ticket
- Intercity: reserved bike spaces available
- High-speed: must be in zip-up bag, wheels removed, handlebars turned

## 1.6 Major Stations

### Rome Termini
- Central city location, walking distance to major sites
- FrecciaClub lounge for Executive passengers
- AVOID: Roma Afragola (out-of-town parkway station)

### Milan Centrale
- Magnificent 1931 terminus, UNESCO-listed
- Best for onward connections (Venice, Florence, Rome, Naples, Genoa)
- FrecciaClub lounge
- NOTE: Paris TGVs arrive at Milan Porta Garibaldi (less convenient, requires metro transfer)

- On Grand Canal, 15-25 min walk to Rialto Bridge/St. Mark's
- Accessible by water transport (vaporetto)
- AVOID: Venezia Mestre (mainland industrial area)

### Florence Santa Maria Novella (SMN)
- City center, walking distance to all attractions
- Some trains use Campo Marte or Rifredi (frequent local connections to SMN)

### Naples Centrale
- Co-located with Circumvesuviana station (Pompeii/Sorrento)
- 30-min walk to ferry terminals (Capri/Ischia)
- AVOID: Napoli Afragola (out of town)

### Turin Porta Nuova
- Historic central terminus for domestic trains
- Paris trains use Turin Porta Susa (underground platforms, also city center)
- Left luggage at Porta Nuova; NOT at Porta Susa

### Pisa
- Pisa Centrale: 2km from Leaning Tower (30-min walk)
- Pisa S. Rossore: 5-10 min walk to tower (some Florence services stop here)

# PART 2: SWITZERLAND

## 2.1 Train Operators

### Swiss Federal Railways (SBB/CFF/FFS) - www.sbb.ch
- Primary national operator
- Operates most mainline InterCity, InterRegional services
- Website NOTE: Default pricing assumes Half Fare Card ownership; select "No discount" for full rates

### Private Operators
- **Matterhorn-Gotthard Bahn (MGB)**: Glacier Express operator
- **Rhatische Bahn (RhB)**: Bernina Express, Albula line operator
- **BLS (Bern-Lotschberg-Simplon)**: Regional services
- **Berner Oberland Bahn**: Interlaken area (covered by Interrail since 2023)
- Multiple smaller regional operators

---

## 2.2 Train Types

### Double-Deck IC/IR Trains
- Major routes: Zurich-Bern-Lausanne-Geneva, Basel-Zurich-Chur
- Upper deck: superior Alpine views (recommended)
- Lower deck: easier access, luggage space
- 1st and 2nd class sections

### ICN Tilting Trains (Pendolino)
- Pininfarina-designed, 200 km/h
- Routes: Geneva-Basel, Lausanne-Zurich, Basel-Lucerne-Lugano
- Restaurant cars available

### Giruno Trains (Latest Generation)
- Low-floor accessible entrances
- Routes: Basel/Zurich-Lugano (via Gotthard Base Tunnel)
- Restaurant cars

### Narrow-Gauge Trains
- Various designs on mountain/scenic routes
- Operated by private companies (MGB, RhB, etc.)
- Often panoramic windows

---

## 2.3 Booking & Tickets

### Key Principle: No Pre-Booking Required
- Regular full-price tickets available in unlimited quantities
- Trains cannot "sell out" at full price
- Buy at station, board next train
- No reservation needed on standard trains

### Reservation Policy
- **Standard trains**: NO reservation needed - just board and sit
- **MANDATORY reservations only**: Bernina Express panoramic cars, Glacier Express, Golden Pass Panoramic
- Regular (non-panoramic) cars on same routes: no reservation needed

### Ticket Types

| Type | Price | Flexibility | Notes |
|------|-------|-------------|-------|
| **Standard Fare** | Full price | Any train, same day | Journey breaks allowed (under 116km same day, over 116km within 10 days) |
| **Supersaver** | Up to 50% off | Specific train only | Non-refundable, risky for connections |
| **Saver Day Pass** | From CHF 52 | All trains, all day | Opens 6 months ahead, unavailable day-of |
| **Half Fare Card** | CHF 120/month | 50% off everything | Worth it if spending >CHF 240 |

### Where to Book
- **www.sbb.ch**: Direct, no fee (beware Half Fare Card default)
- **www.thetrainline.com**: Multiple currencies, small fee
- **www.omio.com**: Multiple currencies, small fee
- All access identical SBB ticketing system

### Money-Saving Hack
German Railways (int.bahn.de) advance Sparpreis tickets from last German stop to Swiss destinations can undercut Swiss domestic pricing. Example: Book Freiburg-Geneva instead of Basel-Geneva. Unused German segment has no penalty.

## 2.4 Swiss Travel Passes

### Swiss Travel Pass (Continuous)
- 3, 4, 8, or 15 consecutive days unlimited travel
- Covers: trains, buses (PostBus), ferries, most mountain railways
- Solid lines on official map: full coverage
- Dotted lines on official map: 50% discount only

### Swiss Travel Pass Flex
- Same coverage as continuous
- 3-15 travel days usable flexibly within one month
- Better for non-consecutive travel days

### Notable EXCLUSIONS from Full Coverage
- Jungfrau Railway (50% discount only)
- Gornergrat Railway (50% discount only)
- Some private mountain railways on dotted lines

### Swiss Half Fare Card
- CHF 120 per month
- 50% off standard fares, Supersaver fares, AND Saver Day Passes
- Economical if planned journey costs exceed CHF 240

### Cost Analysis
- Divide pass price by travel days
- Compare daily cost against expected journey fares
- Interrail/Eurail increasingly competitive (covers most SBB + private operators since 2023 expansion)

## 2.5 Interrail/Eurail Pass Usage in Switzerland

### Coverage
- All SBB trains covered
- Most private operators covered (expanded 2023 to include Berner Oberland Bahn)
- Scenic trains covered but reservation supplements apply

### Supplements for Scenic Trains
- Bernina Express panoramic cars: CHF 32-36 reservation
- Glacier Express: reservation + supplement
- Golden Pass Panoramic: reservation fee
- Regular (non-panoramic) services on same routes: FREE with pass

### Recommended Pass Duration
- 4-day pass: sufficient for London-Switzerland round trip + 2 days Swiss travel

## 2.6 Scenic Routes & Mountain Railways

### Bernina Express (RhB) - UNESCO World Heritage
- **Route**: Chur - St Moritz - Tirano (Italy), 144 km
- **Duration**: ~4 hours
- **Highlights**: 55 tunnels, 196 bridges, Brusio Spiral Viaduct, Lake Poschiavo, Ospizio Bernina summit (2,253m)
- **Best views southbound**: right-hand side
- **Best views northbound**: left-hand side
- **Summer**: 2 daily services (May 2 - Oct 25)
- **Winter**: 1 daily service
- **Panoramic car reservation**: CHF 36 high season, CHF 32 low season
- **Alternative**: Unreserved Allegra unit seats (many prefer these - opening windows for photography, potential driver views from 1st class front seats)
- **Hourly local trains**: Same route, no reservation needed, no panoramic cars
- **Photography tip**: Small opening window in luggage vestibule for reflection-free shots
- **Tirano connection**: Italian Trenord station just meters across town square from Swiss RhB station

### Glacier Express (MGB)
- **Route**: Zermatt - St Moritz, 300 km
- **Mandatory reservation with supplement**
- **Full day journey through mountain passes and gorges**
- **Luxury service with at-seat dining**

### Golden Pass Line
- **Route**: Montreux - Interlaken - Lucerne
- **Narrow-gauge section over Gstaad**
- **New gauge-changing trains with panoramic and Prestige class cars**
- **Frequent daily service**
- **Regular trains available without reservation on same route**

### Jungfraubahn (Jungfraujoch - Top of Europe)
- Rack railway to 3,454m elevation
- From Grindelwald and Lauterbrunnen
- Full day excursion recommended
- NOT fully covered by Swiss Travel Pass (50% discount only)
- Separate ticket required

### Other Mountain Railways
- **Gornergrat**: Views of Matterhorn (50% discount with Swiss Travel Pass)
- **Mt. Pilatus**: Near Lucerne
- **Mt. Titlis**: Near Engelberg
- **Schilthorn**: Murren area (James Bond filming location)

### Mattertal Valley to Zermatt
- Narrow-gauge scenic valley route from Visp
- Best views southbound: left-hand side

## 2.7 Practical Tips

### First vs Second Class
- 1st class costs 75% more than 2nd class
- 1st class: wider seats (2+1 vs 2+2), plusher, fewer passengers
- NO complimentary food, beverages, or additional services on domestic trains
- Yellow stripe above windows/doors identifies 1st class
- Verdict: 2nd class is perfectly adequate for most travelers

### Luggage
- No fees, weight limits, or check-in
- Self-carry, use racks at car ends or overhead storage
- Lockers at major stations in various sizes

### Food & Drink
- Long-distance trains: waiter-service restaurant cars
- Some regional trains: contactless vending machines (hot/cold beverages, snacks)
- Personal food and beverages always permitted
- No prohibition on alcohol

### Bicycles
- Swiss Bike Card required for luggage van transport on most trains
- Purchase at stations or online

### Dogs
- Any size: half 2nd class fare
- Under 30cm shoulder height in carrier: FREE
- Dog Day Card available for longer journeys

### Language
- Station signage: bilingual/trilingual with English pictograms
- Long-distance train announcements: often include English
- Station staff: generally speak English

### Timetable Reliability
- "Trains run like clockwork"
- Connection recommendations: at least 20 minutes for comfortable changes
- Swiss timetable designed around clock-face scheduling (regular intervals)

### Currency
- Swiss Francs (CHF), EUR 1 approximately CHF 1.05
- Many stations accept both CHF and EUR

# PART 3: LONDON TO ITALY ROUTES

## 3.1 Recommended Route: Via Paris (Frecciarossa)

### Journey
1. London St Pancras --> Paris Gare du Nord (Eurostar, 2h20)
2. Cross Paris: Gare du Nord --> Gare de Lyon (RER D, 2 stops, ~15 min)
3. Paris Gare de Lyon --> Milan Centrale (Frecciarossa, ~7h)
4. Milan Centrale --> onward destinations (Frecciarossa, 1-4h)

### Total Journey Times from London
- To Milan: ~13-14 hours
- To Turin: ~12-13 hours
- To Venice: ~15 hours
- To Florence: ~14 hours
- To Rome: ~16 hours
- To Naples: ~17 hours

### Paris Connection
- Allow 2 hours southbound, 2.5 hours northbound (including Eurostar check-in)
- RER Line D: 2 stops between Gare du Nord and Gare de Lyon
- Walking: ~10 minutes between stations
- Alternative: Metro or taxi

### Train Bleu Restaurant
- Historic restaurant at Paris Gare de Lyon
- Listed monument, opened 1901
- Excellent for dining during Paris connection
- Big Ben bar within functions as de facto first-class lounge

---

## 3.2 Alternative Route: Via Paris (TGV)

### Journey
1. London --> Paris (Eurostar, 2h20)
2. Paris Gare de Lyon --> Turin Porta Susa (TGV, ~6-7h)
3. Turin --> onward (Frecciarossa)

### Key Differences from Frecciarossa Route
- TGV arrives at Milan Porta Garibaldi (less convenient) vs Frecciarossa at Milan Centrale
- TGV covered by Interrail/Eurail; Frecciarossa Paris-Milan currently NOT covered
- Two Trenitalia trains = single contract with legal rebooking rights (20-min connection OK)
- TGV+Trenitalia = separate contracts (45-min connection minimum recommended)

## 3.3 Scenic Route: Via Switzerland & Bernina Express

## 3.6 Booking Strategy for London-Italy

### Eurostar (London-Paris)
- Opens: up to 11 months ahead
- Standard: from GBP 51 one-way, GBP 78 return
- Plus (1st class): from GBP 98 one-way, GBP 140 return
- Minimum 30-minute check-in at St Pancras
- All border formalities completed before boarding

### Strategy
- Wait until Italian trains open for sale before purchasing Eurostar
- Book each leg separately for best prices
- Use thetrainline.com or raileurope.com for one-stop shopping

# PART 5: PARIS TO ITALY ROUTES

## 5.1 Paris to Milan/Turin (Direct)

### Frecciarossa (Trenitalia) - RECOMMENDED
- 2 daily departures from Paris Gare de Lyon
- Designed by Pininfarina
- Paris --> Turin: ~6-7 hours
- Paris --> Milan Centrale: ~7-8 hours
- Classes: Standard (from EUR 35), Business (from EUR 49), Executive (from EUR 165)
- Arrives Milan CENTRALE (better for connections)
- Stops at Lyon Part Dieu

### TGV (SNCF)
- 3 daily departures from Paris Gare de Lyon
- Paris --> Turin: ~6-7 hours
- Paris --> Milan Porta Garibaldi: ~7-8 hours
- Classes: 2nd (from EUR 39), 1st (from EUR 59)
- Arrives Milan PORTA GARIBALDI (less convenient, metro needed)
- Bypasses Lyon on high-speed line

### Frecciarossa vs TGV Comparison

| Feature | Frecciarossa | TGV |
|---------|-------------|-----|
| Milan station | Centrale (better) | Porta Garibaldi (worse) |
| Comfort | Newer, more glamorous | Functional, aging |
| Executive class | Yes (10 luxury seats) | No equivalent |
| Food included | Business: yes | No |
| Interrail/Eurail | NOT covered (may change 2026) | Covered (EUR 30+ reservation) |
| Connection rights | Two Trenitalia = single contract | Separate contracts |
| Child fares | Under 14 | Under 12 |

### Scenic Highlights
- 300 km/h dash through rural France
- Crossing the Rhone
- Scenic meander through French Alps
- 13.6 km Frejus Rail Tunnel (opened 1871)
- Alpine views on Italian side

### Luggage Rules (TGV specific, 2024+)
- 1 hand luggage (40x30x15cm) + 2 suitcases (90x70x50cm)
- Or 1 hand luggage + 1 suitcase + 1 specific item
- Must carry yourself in one go
- EUR 50 fee if exceeded
- Bikes in zip-up covers under 120x90cm: free

## 7.2 Money-Saving Strategies

1. **Book early**: Fares vary like air fares - cheapest available months ahead
2. **German Railways hack**: Book from last German stop to Swiss/Italian destination via int.bahn.de for cheaper Sparpreis fares
3. **Saver Day Pass (Switzerland)**: From CHF 52 for unlimited daily travel
4. **Swiss Half Fare Card**: If spending >CHF 240, the CHF 120 card saves money
5. **Italia In Tour**: EUR 35 for 3 days unlimited regional Italian trains
6. **Compare Trenitalia vs Italo**: Use thetrainline.com for side-by-side
7. **Regional trains in Italy**: Fixed prices, no advance booking advantage
8. **Bernina Express Allegra unit**: Skip panoramic car reservation, ride unreserved for free with pass

## 7.3 Interrail/Eurail Key Considerations

### Covered
- Eurostar (passholder reservation required, EUR 30, limited seats)
- TGV-Lyria (reservation EUR 10-15)
- TGV Paris-Italy (reservation EUR 30+)
- All Trenitalia services (Frecciarossa reservation EUR 13, IC EUR 3, regional free)
- SBB Swiss trains (free, no reservation)
- Most Swiss private operators (since 2023 expansion)
- EuroCity Switzerland-Italy
- Bernina Express (reservation for panoramic only, CHF 32-36)

### NOT Covered
- Frecciarossa Paris-Milan/Turin (may change 2026)
- Italo/NTV trains
- Circumvesuviana Railway
- Some specific mountain railways in full (50% discount via Swiss Travel Pass)

## 7.4 Environmental Note
- Train travel generates approximately 80% less CO2 than flying to Italy

# PART 8: COUNTRY-SPECIFIC QUIRKS & WARNINGS

## Italy Quirks
1. **Validate regional tickets**: Green machines at platform entrance (online tickets exempt)
2. **Italian place names required**: Trenitalia.com uses Firenze not Florence, etc.
3. **Porta Garibaldi vs Centrale**: Paris TGVs arrive at the wrong Milan station
4. **Afragola stations**: Avoid Roma Afragola and Napoli Afragola (out-of-town parkway stops)
5. **Mestre vs Santa Lucia**: Venice Mestre is mainland; Santa Lucia is on the island
6. **Sicily delays**: Overnight sleepers regularly 30-90 minutes late
7. **Tap & Tap**: Contactless payments only on selected routes
8. **Trenitalia Pass vs Interrail**: Trenitalia Pass includes reservations; Interrail does not
9. **"Silenzio" cars on Frecciarossa**: Window silhouettes obscure views - avoid if sightseeing

## Switzerland Quirks
1. **SBB.ch Half Fare Card default**: Website assumes you have one; select "No discount" for real prices
2. **No first class lounges**: Zurich and Geneva lounges closed
3. **Supersaver risk**: Train-specific, no refunds - risky if depending on connecting services
4. **Saver Day Pass unavailable day-of**: Must be booked in advance
5. **Panoramic vs regular**: Same scenic routes available on regular trains without reservation
6. **Dotted vs solid lines**: Swiss Travel Pass map - dotted = 50% only, solid = full coverage
7. **Clock-face scheduling**: Swiss trains run at regular intervals (easy to remember)
8. **Bernina Express peak crowds**: Station access control at key locations during peak periods

## Cross-Border Quirks
1. **No through London-Switzerland tickets**: Always split bookings
2. **Crossing Paris**: Different stations (Gare du Nord arrival, Gare de Lyon departure)
3. **Eurostar check-in**: 30-minute minimum at St Pancras
4. **Connection protection**: Railteam/HOTNAT covers some cross-operator delays but not all
5. **Currency differences**: EUR in France/Italy, CHF in Switzerland, GBP in UK
6. **Booking opens at different times**: Eurostar 11 months, TGV 6 months, Italian 4 months
