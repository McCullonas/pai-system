### Global Pass - Flexi (travel days spread across validity period)

| Option | Adult 2nd | Youth 2nd | Senior 2nd |
|--------|-----------|-----------|------------|
| 4 days in 1 month | EUR 283 | EUR 212 | EUR 255 |
| 5 days in 1 month | EUR 318 | EUR 239 | EUR 286 |
| 7 days in 1 month | EUR 381 | EUR 286 | EUR 343 |
| 10 days in 2 months | EUR 447 | EUR 335 | EUR 402 |
| 15 days in 2 months | EUR 553 | EUR 415 | EUR 498 |

### Global Pass - Continuous (unbroken travel period)

| Option | Adult 2nd | Youth 2nd | Senior 2nd |
|--------|-----------|-----------|------------|
| 15 days | EUR 476 | EUR 357 | EUR 428 |
| 22 days | EUR 586 | EUR 440 | EUR 527 |
| 1 month | EUR 696 | EUR 522 | EUR 626 |
| 2 months | EUR 826 | EUR 620 | EUR 743 |
| 3 months | EUR 956 | EUR 717 | EUR 860 |

**1st class pricing**: approximately 25-30% higher than 2nd class equivalents.

### Age Categories

- **Youth**: Under 28 on pass start date
- **Adult**: Ages 28-59
- **Senior**: Ages 60+
- **Child**: Under 12 travel FREE with adult passholder (max 2 children per adult). Children 4-11 still pay reservation fees.
- **Under 4**: Travel free without pass (varies by country, some allow under 5-6)

### One-Country Passes

Available for individual countries with options of 3, 4, 5, 6, or 8 days within 1 month (flexi format). Prices vary by country. Check at thetrainline.com/passes, interrail.eu, or raileurope.com.

## 4. Activation & Validity Rules

### Mobile Pass Activation Steps

1. Download Rail Planner app from interrail.eu
2. Load pass using pass number from confirmation email
3. When ready to travel, activate by entering passport number and desired start date
4. Can change activation date anytime until 23:59 the day before (CET)
5. Once activated at 00:00 CET, cannot change or refund

### Continuous Pass Validity

- 15 days continuous = 15 consecutive calendar days
- 1 month continuous = full calendar month (e.g., July 10 - August 9)
- 2 months continuous = full 2-calendar-month period
- 3 months continuous = full 3-calendar-month period

### Flexi Pass Validity

- "4 days in 1 month" = 4 travel days usable anytime within 1 calendar month from activation
- Travel days do NOT need to be consecutive
- "Spending" a day = midnight to midnight of unlimited travel
- Example: 5-days-in-1-month pass activated July 10: can use travel days on July 11, 15, 17, 23, August 2 (any combination within the 1-month window)

### Mobile Connectivity Requirement

Connect the app to internet via WiFi or mobile data at least once every 3 days to keep the pass valid. It works offline in between for showing passes to conductors.

## 5. Travel Days vs Calendar Days

### Key Distinction

- One travel day = midnight to midnight (24 hours) of unlimited train travel
- Unlimited trains, unlimited kilometers within that window
- NOT limited to one train per day

### Flexi Pass Travel Days

- "Spend" each day independently on dates of your choosing
- Days do not need to be consecutive
- Provides flexibility to skip travel on some days

### Continuous Pass Travel Days

- Consecutive calendar days, no choice
- Cannot "save" unused days; once day passes, it's consumed

### Overnight Train Rule (Flexi Passes Only)

- Overnight train departing before midnight, arriving next day, with NO train changes after midnight = counts as 1 travel day (the departure date)
- Does not matter what time train arrives next day
- Example: Vienna 19:42 to Bucharest 16:06 next day = 1 travel day dated first day
- Can take other trains same day before boarding the sleeper
- BUT arrival day must still fall within overall pass validity window
- If continuing on a different train after midnight, would need 2 days

## 7. Home Country Restrictions

### The Two Inbound/Outbound Days Rule

- Global Interrail pass does NOT give unlimited travel in your home country
- You receive 2 outbound/inbound days
- One journey OUT at trip start, one journey BACK at trip end
- Can involve multiple trains but should be continuous journey from home to border/airport/ferry port or return

### How It Works

- Journey can include multiple train segments (e.g., home station to London to Eurostar to Brussels to Cologne = 1 outbound day)
- On flexi passes, inbound/outbound day counts as one of your travel days (not additional)
- Should not abuse by traveling around home country all day
- Can split: first inbound/outbound on Day 1, second on final day, or use on any two dates during validity

### For UK Residents

- Eurostar London-Continent counts as home country travel and triggers inbound/outbound day
- Example: Dundee to Edinburgh to London to Eurostar to Brussels to Cologne = 1 outbound day

## 8. How to Use the Pass (Mobile & Paper)

### Mobile Pass: Step-by-Step

1. **Finding trains**: Use Planner section in Rail Planner app to search train times
2. **Saving journeys**: Click "Save journey" to add to your trip folder (no obligation to use)
3. **Toggling onto pass**: When boarding, slide toggle to activate journey on your pass. App asks to confirm using one pass day if first train that day
4. **Showing to conductor**: Open app, navigate to "My pass," click "Show ticket." QR code appears for scanning
5. **Un-toggling**: Can un-toggle before midnight; cannot after midnight on travel day
6. **Toggling does NOT equal reserving**: These are completely separate actions

### Mobile Pass Troubleshooting

- **QR code not appearing**: Go to "My Trip," deselect train, retoggle onto pass, try "Show Ticket" again
- **App denies valid pass**: Put phone in airplane mode (stops server contact in poor reception), restart app
- **Lost/stolen/broken phone**: Load pass onto new phone using pass number and surname, select "Move pass" (can only do once; beyond that contact Interrail customer services)

### Manual Journey Addition

If Rail Planner cannot find a train: scroll to bottom of search and click "Can't find your journey? Add it manually to your pass." Enter origin, destination, time, and optional train number.

### Hard-Copy Pass Usage

- Write date in one box per travel day in indelible black or blue pen
- Use boxes sequentially for flexi passes
- Complete travel diary for every journey (separate entries for each segment)
- Conductors typically ask you to complete missing entries rather than fining

### What Inspectors Check

- Valid pass
- Passport (occasionally, not always)
- Travel diary reflects the journey (hard-copy)
- Journeys properly toggled on mobile pass
- For mobile: may ask you to scroll down from QR code to verify specific journey is recorded

### Multiple Travelers on One Phone

- Create duplicate trips for each person
- Rename trips to identify ownership
- Save journeys to multiple trips simultaneously
- Toggle each person's journeys individually when boarding (no batch process)
- Better practice: give each person their own phone with their own pass

## 9. Reservation Requirements by Country

### Green Zone: Travel Freely (reservation usually optional)

**Ireland, Netherlands, Belgium, Luxembourg, Switzerland, Germany, Austria, Denmark, most Central & Eastern Europe**
- Seat reservation usually optional
- No fee or minimal fee (EUR 3-5)
- Can board trains without reservation
- Cannot "sell out"

**Exceptions within green zone**:
- International trains TO/FROM France require mandatory reservation with fee
- Eurostar (formerly Thalys) Brussels-Amsterdam/Cologne require mandatory reservation with fee
- Railjet/Eurocity to Italy sometimes have surcharge
- Poland, Slovakia, Hungary, Romania require reservations on inter-city trains (EUR 1-3)
- Swiss scenic trains (Glacier Express, Bernina Express) require seat reservation/supplement

### Red Zone: Plan Ahead (mandatory reservations)

**France, Italy, Spain, Portugal, Sweden**
- ALL inter-city trains are fully reserved (like airplanes)
- Mandatory passholder reservation for every inter-city journey
- Budget EUR 10+ per train
- Examples: Italian Frecciarossa EUR 13, French TGV EUR 10-20, formerly-Thalys EUR 15-25, Paris-Milan EUR 31, Paris-Barcelona EUR 34

### Quota Limitations

- Most trains: no quota, passholders guaranteed seats if seats exist
- LIMITED quota (can sell out for passholders): Eurostar London, formerly-Thalys Paris-Brussels-Amsterdam, Paris-Turin-Milan TGVs, Paris-Barcelona TGVs
- French domestic TGV, TGV-Lyria, Spanish/Italian high-speed usually have availability even last-minute

### Avoiding Reservation Fees

Choose slower regional trains instead of high-speed:
- Rome-Florence: Frecciarossa EUR 13 OR InterCity EUR 3 OR regional trains no fee
- Brussels-Amsterdam: formerly-Thalys EUR 22 OR EuroCity Direct no reservation (2h08 vs 1h49)
- Use int.bahn.de timetable, select "Local transport only" to find no-fee alternatives

## 10. How to Make Reservations

**CRITICAL**: "Pass and reservations are totally, utterly, completely separate." Activating/adding train to pass does NOT equal making a reservation. Must have BOTH valid pass AND valid reservation for trains requiring reservations.

### Method 1: Train Operator Websites (no booking fee, best option)

| Operator | Website | Coverage |
|----------|---------|----------|
| DB (Germany) | int.bahn.de | German domestic + international to Amsterdam, Brussels, Prague, Warsaw, Copenhagen, Switzerland |
| OBB (Austria) | oebb.at | Nightjet sleepers, Railjet Austria trains |
| SJ (Sweden) | sj.se | Swedish trains, Copenhagen-Stockholm, Stockholm-Oslo, Hamburg-Stockholm sleeper |
| CD (Czech) | cd.cz | Czech trains + international from Prague |
| PKP (Poland) | intercity.pl | Polish trains + Berlin-Warsaw/Krakow |

### Method 2: Official Interrail Reservation Service

- URL: interrail.eu/en/book-reservations (requires pass number)
- Covers most trains in Western/Central Europe, Eurostar
- NOT available for: Great Britain, Ireland, Portugal, Finland, Bulgaria, Greece, Turkey
- EUR 2 booking fee per reservation PLUS reservation fee
- Available until 3 hours before departure
- No seat selection offered
- Usually e-ticketed

### Method 3: Belgian Railways

- URL: b-europe.com/EN/Booking/Pass
- Covers Eurostar and formerly-Thalys
- Can check passholder availability WITHOUT pass number first
- EUR 4 booking fee (vs EUR 2 on Interrail service)

### Method 4: Rail Europe

- URL: raileurope.com
- Select "Add rail pass" option
- Can book passholder reservations for Western European routes including France, Italy

### Method 5: UK Phone Agencies

- **International Rail**: 0844 248 248 3
- **Trainseurope**: 0871 700 7722 (Monday-Friday office hours)
- **German Railways UK**: 00 49 (0)30 311 68 29 04 (Mon-Fri 08:30-20:00, Sat-Sun 09:00-13:00 UK time; 1.5% phone booking fee)

### Method 6: In-Person at Stations

- Visit station ticket office before or day of travel
- Show rail pass, request reservation
- Usually can book up to departure time
- Write down destination/details if language barrier

## 11. Eurostar-Specific Information

### Passholder Fares

- 2nd class pass: EUR 35 Standard (London to Paris/Lille/Brussels/Calais)
- 1st class pass: EUR 40 Plus
- Slightly more for London to Rotterdam/Amsterdam
- Fixed price (not variable)

### Availability

- Controlled by quota (limited passholder places)
- Wide availability if booking well in advance
- Reasonable availability even day-before/day-of
- Recommendation: check Eurostar availability BEFORE buying pass

### Booking Eurostar

- Via interrail.eu/en/book-reservations
- Via b-europe.com/EN/Booking/Pass
- Mobile pass numbers may need conversion to pass cover number at eurail.com/en/book-reservations#/generatePassCoverNumber

### Check-In Requirements

- Arrive 30 minutes before departure (15 minutes for premium 1st class)
- Airline-style security screening

## 12. Overnight & Sleeper Trains

### Nightjet Supplement Prices (starting, vary like airfares)

| Accommodation | Starting Price |
|---------------|---------------|
| Seat | EUR 14 |
| Couchette (6-berth) | EUR 34 |
| Couchette (4-berth) | EUR 44 |
| Sleeper (3-berth) | EUR 54 |
| Sleeper (2-berth) | EUR 74 |
| Single sleeper | EUR 114 |
| Deluxe 2-berth sleeper | EUR 94 |
| Deluxe single sleeper | EUR 134 |

### Class Requirements

- Only 2nd class pass needed for ALL Nightjet accommodation types (even single/deluxe sleepers)
- Eastern Europe: 1st class required for single sleepers; 2nd class OK for double/triple
- Italy: 2nd class for all types

### Accommodation Types

- **Couchettes**: Basic padded bunks, sheet/rug/pillow, 4 or 6 per compartment ("youth hostel on rails")
- **Sleepers**: Proper beds with washbasin, 1/2/3 beds per compartment ("hotels on rails")
- Berth types: single (alone), double (shared with same-gender), triple (2 others)

### Overnight Train + Flexi Pass Rules

- Single overnight train (depart before midnight, arrive next day, no changes after midnight) = 1 travel day (departure date)
- Unlimited travel on departure day can include other trains before sleeper
- If continuing by train on arrival day, needs separate travel day
- Both departure and arrival days must fall within pass validity

### Other Overnight Services

- **European Sleeper**: Brussels/Amsterdam to Berlin and Prague (europeansleeper.eu)
- **Paris-Barcelona sleeper**: Runs seasonally

## 14. Interrail vs Point-to-Point Tickets

### Cost-Per-Day Calculation

| Pass | Cost/Day |
|------|----------|
| 5 days/1 month adult | EUR 64/day |
| 10 days/2 months adult | EUR 45/day |
| 5 days/1 month youth | EUR 48/day |
| 10 days/2 months youth | EUR 34/day |

### When Point-to-Point Wins

- Booking 2-3 fixed journeys on known dates months in advance
- Pre-booked accommodation at specific locations
- Comfortable with no-refund, no-changes terms
- Advance-purchase fares significantly cheaper than pass cost
- Short hops (Vienna-Bratislava EUR 12, Florence-Pisa EUR 9)

### When Interrail Wins

- Short-notice travel
- Flexibility valued (change plans without penalty)
- Extensive itinerary with multiple journeys
- Insurance against disruptions: can reroute easily with pass
- High-speed/premium trains: pass often cheaper than full-flex fares

### Factor in Reservation Fees

- France, Italy, Spain: budget EUR 10+ per train
- Benelux, Switzerland, Germany, Austria, Denmark, Eastern Europe: usually EUR 0-5 or optional
- Sleepers: EUR 34-114+ per berth
- Eurostar: EUR 35-40
- Example: 5-days-in-1-month pass (EUR 318) + 5 x EUR 10 French reservations (EUR 50) = EUR 368 total

## 15. European Train Travel: General Booking Guide

### Critical Fact

No single website sells tickets for all European trains. Each national operator has separate ticketing.

### Primary Booking Options

**Aggregator sites (link multiple operators)**:
- **raileurope.com** - covers France, Spain, Italy, Germany, Austria, Switzerland, GB, Benelux. Can book multi-operator journeys as single transactions. Small booking fees.
- **thetrainline.com** - similar coverage and capabilities

**National operator websites**:

| Country | Operator | Website |
|---------|----------|---------|
| France | SNCF | sncf-connect.com |
| Germany | DB | int.bahn.de |
| Italy | Trenitalia | trenitalia.com |
| Spain | Renfe | renfe.com |
| Austria | OBB | oebb.at |
| Switzerland | SBB | sbb.ch |
| Netherlands | NS | ns.nl |
| Czech Republic | CD | cd.cz |
| Hungary | MAV | mavcsoport.hu |

**Tip**: Check both starting AND destination operator websites for the same international journey. Prices can differ significantly for identical trains.

### Booking Windows

- Germany, Austria, Switzerland: 11 months ahead
- France: typically 2-4 months
- Spain: sometimes only weeks ahead
- Italy (Trenitalia): loads trains incrementally, creating gaps

Major timetable changes: 2nd Saturday in December and 2nd Saturday in June.

### Breaking Down Journeys

Many longer journeys cannot be booked as single transactions across operators. Solution: purchase separate tickets for each stage. Example: Nice to Milan needs separate Nice-Ventimiglia (SNCF) and Ventimiglia-Milan (Trenitalia) tickets.

### Payment Notes

Some national operator websites struggle with overseas payment cards. raileurope.com and thetrainline.com specifically designed for international users and accept overseas cards readily.

## 17. First Class vs Second Class

### 2nd Class

- "Absolutely fine for most travellers"
- Modern European trains offer comfortable seating
- Typically 2+2 seating layout

### 1st Class Advantages

- Wider seats with more leg room (typically 2+1 seating)
- Fewer passengers per coach
- Face-to-face tables for two in many trains
- Some premium trains include meals/drinks (Eurostar Plus/Premier, Spanish AVE, Lyria)

### Cost Reality

On trains with dynamic pricing, 1st class fares sometimes only slightly exceed 2nd class. Upgrades as low as EUR 5 are sometimes available.

### Sleeper Trains

Class designation is irrelevant for sleepers. Comfort depends on accommodation type (seat, couchette, sleeper), not class. 2nd class couchettes offer more comfort/security than 1st class seats.

## 19. Station Tips & Connection Times

### Connection Time Recommendations

- Minimum 40 minutes recommended for transfers (especially on separate tickets)
- On through-bookings: legally entitled to later onward travel if delay causes missed connection
- On separate tickets: risk of missed connections lies with passenger

### Paris Station Changes

Paris has multiple major stations (Gare du Nord, Est, de Lyon, Montparnasse, d'Austerlitz, Saint-Lazare). Connections between them require metro, bus, or taxi.
- Gare du Nord to Gare de l'Est: approximately 7 minutes / 500m walk
- Use ratp.fr for metro routes

### Brussels Midi

Single main hub for all long-distance trains. Connections simple, requiring minutes.

### Arriving at Stations

- Eurostar: 30 minutes before departure required
- Most other trains: 15 minutes before departure suffices

### Station Facilities

Most major European stations have:
- Luggage lockers
- First class lounges
- Hotels nearby
- Wheelchair/accessibility services

## 21. Common Mistakes & Warnings

1. **Confusing pass with reservation**: Toggling train onto pass does NOT book/reserve it
2. **Forgetting to fill travel diary** (hard-copy pass): Risk of fine
3. **Not accounting for reservation fees**: Can significantly increase pass cost in France/Italy/Spain
4. **Not checking country-specific coverage**: Some operators/routes not covered (Italo, Ouigo, Avlo, Circumvesuviana)
5. **Abusing inbound/outbound days**: Should be for home-to-border travel only
6. **Using pass for short hops**: Vienna-Bratislava EUR 12, Florence-Pisa EUR 9 cheaper than extending pass
7. **Forgetting mobile phone power**: Tickets on phone need battery
8. **Activating too early**: Purchasing months in advance and setting future activation date then forgetting
9. **Over-using home country days**: Only 2 inbound/outbound days allowed
10. **Missing reservation requirements**: Boarding reserved-only trains without booking
11. **Assuming passes cover everything**: Metros, trams, private operators, budget subsidiaries excluded
12. **Not checking Eurostar quota**: Passholder places limited
13. **Booking too far ahead** before booking windows open (varies 2-11 months by operator)
14. **Assuming one website covers all trains** across Europe
15. **Not allowing sufficient connection time** (minimum 40 minutes recommended)
16. **Not comparing prices** across multiple operator websites for international journeys
17. **Forgetting to toggle journeys on mobile pass** before conductor arrives

## 22. Smart Strategies & Budget Tips

1. **Activate flexi pass day-of** (not months ahead) to avoid accidental wasted validity
2. **Check Eurostar availability before buying pass**: Limited passholder quota
3. **Use int.bahn.de "Local transport only"** filter to find free alternatives to high-speed
4. **Book reservations in advance** for France/Italy/Spain: prices increase closer to date
5. **Keep phone charged**: Use powerbank as backup
6. **Get travel insurance**: Essential for disruptions and emergencies
7. **Use Curve card**: Avoid foreign transaction fees
8. **Load eSIM**: Stay connected without expensive roaming
9. **Buy European Rail Timetable** for extensive touring
10. **Stay near stations** for short visits: simplifies logistics, bag storage usually free
11. **Advance purchase** point-to-point tickets: significant savings available
12. **Check 1st class pricing**: Sometimes only marginally more than 2nd
13. **Travel mid-week** and in shoulder season (February, September) for cheaper fares
14. **Luxembourg offers free 2nd class train travel** within the country
15. **Break down complex journeys** into bookable segments for best prices
16. **Use Lille as connection point** to avoid Paris station changes
