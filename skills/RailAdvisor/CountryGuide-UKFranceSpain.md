# 1. UNITED KINGDOM

## 1.1 Network Structure

Britain's rail network operates through **over 20 private train companies** functioning as a unified system called **National Rail**. Tickets purchased from any operator are valid across the entire network regardless of which company operates each segment.

## 1.2 Key Train Operators

| Operator | Key Routes | First Class Perks |
|----------|-----------|-------------------|
| **LNER** | London-York-Edinburgh (East Coast) | Light meals & wine included |
| **Avanti West Coast** | London-Manchester-Glasgow (West Coast) | Light meals & wine included |
| **GWR** | London-Bath-Bristol-Cardiff-Cornwall | Complimentary tea/coffee |
| **CrossCountry** | Birmingham hub, cross-country routes | Complimentary tea/coffee |
| **East Midlands** | London-Sheffield-Nottingham | Light meals & wine included |

## 1.4 Fare Types

| Fare Type | Flexibility | Price Example (London-Manchester) | Restrictions |
|-----------|------------|-----------------------------------|-------------|
| **Anytime** | Full flexibility, any train | GBP 184+ | None; refundable |
| **Off-Peak** | Any off-peak train | GBP 68-98 | No weekday peaks (typically 15:00-19:00) |
| **Advance** | Single specified train only | GBP 20+ | No refunds; limited/no changes; seat reserved |

**Ticket validity:**
- One-way Anytime: valid for 2 days
- Return: outward 5 days, return within 1 month

## 1.5 Booking Advice

**When to book:** Train ticket booking opens **8-12 weeks ahead**. Advance fares are cheapest but train-specific.

**Where to book (no booking fee):**
- **tfw.wales** (Transport for Wales) - recommended, no fee
- **nationalrail.co.uk** - official timetables and fares
- **thetrainline.com** - charges fees but has fare alerts

**WARNING:** Do not book when only expensive Anytime/Off-Peak fares are available. Wait for Advance fares to appear (8-12 weeks ahead).

**Split-ticketing:** On cross-country routes (e.g., Plymouth-Glasgow, Bristol-Scarborough), check **trainsplit.com** for significant savings by splitting journeys into multiple tickets.

## 1.6 Reservation Requirements

- Reservations are **optional on all British trains** (except overnight sleepers)
- Advance tickets include automatic reservations
- Free reservations available through operator websites for flexible tickets
- Recommended on busy long-distance routes

## 1.7 Rail Passes & Discount Cards

### BritRail Pass (overseas visitors only)
- Unlimited travel on all operators
- Typically costs about the same as Off-Peak flexible tickets bought on the day
- Compare against point-to-point Advance tickets before purchasing

### Railcards (GBP 35/year or GBP 80/3 years)
| Card | Discount | Notes |
|------|----------|-------|
| 16-25 Railcard | 34% | Minimum GBP 12 peak charge |
| Senior (60+) | 34% | - |
| Family & Friends | 34% adult, 60% child | - |
| Two Together | 33% | For paired travelers |

### All-Line Rail Rover
- 7-day: GBP 625 / 14-day: GBP 949 (standard class)
- Weekday restriction at major London terminals before 10:00

### Accessibility Discount
- Wheelchair users or registered blind: automatic 34% on Anytime fares without railcard
- One companion also receives 34%

## 1.8 Scenic Routes

- **West Highland Line** (Scotland) - Scotland's most scenic route
- **Settle & Carlisle Line** - Dramatic viaduct crossings
- **Caledonian Sleeper** - London to Scotland overnight

## 1.9 Practical Tips

**Luggage:** Left luggage at major London terminals, Birmingham, Edinburgh, Glasgow, Leeds, Liverpool, Manchester. Most smaller stations lack facilities.

**Bicycles:** Most trains carry free or small fee with advance reservation. Tandems rarely accommodated.

**Food:** First class includes meals/drinks on LNER, Avanti, East Midlands. GWR and CrossCountry offer tea/coffee in first.

**Platform info:** Numbers posted only 10-30 minutes before departure at major terminals.

**Upgrades:** **Seatfrog** app allows bidding for budget first class upgrades on LNER, GWR, Avanti, EMR, TransPennine, CrossCountry.

**PlusBus:** Add unlimited bus travel in origin/destination city for GBP 2-3 extra.

## 1.10 Quirks & Warnings

- Route restrictions on some tickets (check "via" routing)
- "Travelling short" (alighting early) violates conditions of carriage
- Peak-hour restrictions: London off-peak typically restricts before 09:30-10:00
- **realtimetrains.co.uk** shows live train formations and platform assignments
- **brfares.com** shows detailed fare restrictions

# 2. EUROSTAR (London to Paris & Continental Europe)

## 2.1 Service Overview

| Detail | Specification |
|--------|--------------|
| Route | London St Pancras to Paris Gare du Nord |
| Journey time | 2h 15m (non-stop) |
| Distance | 492 km (305 miles) |
| Maximum speed | 300 km/h (186 mph) |
| Frequency | Hourly or twice hourly, 7 days/week |
| No service | Christmas Day (December 25) |
| On-time performance | 90% within 15 minutes |

## 2.2 Fares & Classes

| Class | One-Way | Return | Includes |
|-------|---------|--------|----------|
| **Standard** (2nd) | from GBP 51 | from GBP 78 | Seat, WiFi, power |
| **Plus** (1st leisure) | from GBP 98 | from GBP 140 | Light meal, drinks |
| **Premier** (1st business) | GBP 325 | GBP 490 | 3-course meal, champagne, lounge |

**Children:** Under 12 get 30-50% discount. Under 4 travel free without seat. Ages 12-16 unaccompanied need parental consent forms.

**Cheapest fares:** Appear Monday midday to Thursday midnight, plus Saturday midday to midnight. Saturday overnight stays typically required for best return rates.

## 2.3 Booking

- **Booking window:** Up to 11 months ahead
- **Book at:** eurostar.com or sncf-connect.com (no fees)
- **Manage booking:** Use 6-character reference to change seats, check train type
- **Phone:** 03432 186 186 (UK), +44 1233 617575 (overseas). GBP 10 fee per phone booking (not Premier)

### Refund/Change Policy
| Class | Refund | Changes |
|-------|--------|---------|
| Standard/Plus | Until 7 days before, GBP 25 fee | Until 1 hour before, no fee (fare difference applies) |
| Premier | Until 48h AFTER departure | Full flexibility |

## 2.4 Check-In Procedures

**Arrive:** 60-90 minutes before departure recommended.
**Check-in opens:** ~90 minutes before. **Closes:** 30 minutes before (15-20 for Premier).

### London St Pancras Process
1. EES biometric scanning (from Oct 2025-Apr 2026)
2. Ticket scan (QR code at gates)
3. Security X-ray (shoes stay on)
4. UK passport check
5. French passport control (e-gates available)
6. Departure lounge (shops, cafes, charging)

**Premier:** Exclusive lounge with complimentary drinks, spirits, snacks.

### Paris Gare du Nord Process
1. Escalators opposite platform 17 to first floor
2. Ticket scan at gates near platform 3
3. French police passport check
4. UK Border Force check (e-gates available)
5. Security X-ray
6. Departure lounge

**Boarding via footbridges:** Porte A = cars 11-18 (rear). Porte B = cars 1-10 (London end).

## 2.5 Train Fleet

| Train | Units | Features |
|-------|-------|----------|
| **e320** (Siemens, 2015) | 17 | 16 cars, 1st class at each end, WiFi, power, 2+1 first class |
| **e300** (refurbished 1994) | 8 | 18 cars, 1st class mid-train, comparable interiors |

**Tall passenger tip:** e320 seat edges extend 2 inches by pulling out (all classes).

**Identify your train:** Use "View or change seats" in Manage Your Booking. e320 = 16 cars. e300 = 18 cars.

## 2.6 Onboard Facilities

### Food & Drink
- **Premier:** 3-course hot meal, champagne (Monopol brut), wine, beer. Breakfast before 10:15, lunch/dinner after.
- **Plus:** Cold light meal with tea/coffee (AM) or lunch with wine (PM).
- **Standard:** Cafe-bar cars (cars 8-9 on e320; cars 6-13 on e300). Credit cards and cash accepted. Paris metro tickets available at bar.
- **BYO:** Allowed including wine and beer. Suggested limit: 1 wine bottle or 4 beer cans for consumption; unlimited unopened transport.

### Power & Connectivity
- Power sockets and USB at all seats (alternating UK 3-pin and EU 2-pin)
- Free WiFi throughout
- Mobile reception works throughout including Channel Tunnel
- Streaming at onboard.eurostar.com

### Luggage
- **Limit:** 2 large items (max 85cm per dimension) + 1 small carry-on per person
- No weight restrictions
- Items under 85cm: overhead racks. Larger: entrance suitcase racks
- Must be labeled with passenger name
- **In practice:** Guitars, 1m tubes with posters etc. carried without issues
- **Skis/snowboard:** One pair any length permitted
- **Musical instruments:** Under 85cm free; over 85cm may need paid seat
- **Checked baggage service:** Available but items only available 24h after arrival

### Prohibited Items
- Knives with blades over 3 inches
- Ice axes (since 2018)
- Firearms and explosives
- Pets (except guide dogs) - unlike most European trains

Eurostar arrives at **Gare du Nord**. Onward trains depart from other stations:

| Destination | Paris Station | Transfer Method | Time |
|-------------|--------------|-----------------|------|
| Gare de l'Est | 10 min walk | Walk | 10 min |

| Gare Montparnasse | Metro | Metro Line 4 | 25-35 min |

## 2.9 Alternative London-Paris Routes

1. **Train & ferry via Dover-Calais:** ~9 hours, may be cheaper than last-minute Eurostar
2. **Overnight Portsmouth-Caen:** Depart London Waterloo ~18:00, Brittany Ferries overnight with en-suite cabin, arrive Paris St Lazare ~11:05
3. **Stena Line Harwich-Hoek van Holland:** Overnight ferry with cabin, WiFi, satellite TV

## 2.10 Environmental Credentials
- Eurostar: 11 kg CO2 per passenger
- Flight: 122 kg CO2 per passenger (90% more)
- Upper-atmosphere aircraft emissions cause ~2.7x climate damage vs. ground level

## 3.7 Charles de Gaulle Airport Rail Access

**RER Line B (Recommended):**
- Stations at Terminal 1 and Terminal 2
- Every 6-15 minutes, ~50 min to central Paris, ~EUR 10
- No reservation needed

**Direct TGV from CDG:**
- Direct to destinations across France
- Allow at least 3 hours between flight landing and TGV departure
- If no convenient CDG departure, use RER to Paris (EUR 10) then book from central station at least 4 hours after flight

## 3.8 Practical Tips

### Luggage
- No check-in; carry yourself
- TGV InOui limit: 1 hand luggage + 2 suitcases (or 1 suitcase + 1 specific item)
- Hand luggage max: 40 x 30 x 15 cm
- Suitcases max: 90 x 70 x 50 cm
- EUR 50 fee if exceeding limits
- Left luggage lockers at Gare du Nord, Austerlitz, Lyon (bags X-rayed)

### Bicycles
- Free on regional/suburban trains
- EUR 10 on some Intercites de Nuit & TGV routes
- Free in zip-up bike bag on other TGVs

### Food & Drink
- Cafe-bars on long-distance trains (tea, coffee, wine, beer, snacks)
- No restaurant cars on domestic trains currently
- **BYO food and drink (including wine) allowed**
- Pre-ordering available on Intercites via sncf-connect.com
- **Train Bleu restaurant** at Gare de Lyon: famous 1903 art nouveau, book at le-train-bleu.com

### Seating Strategy
- Upper deck on TGV Duplex: superior views over sound barriers
- Forward-facing available on Atlantique, Est, Italy routes via sncf-connect.com
- NOT available on TGV Duplex or Lille-Lyon-Avignon routes
- Table seating (4) guarantees at least one forward-facing seat

### Ticket Validation (Compostage)
- Yellow machines at platform entrances (being phased out)
- Print-at-home/e-tickets do NOT require validation
- Non-validated paper tickets incur fines

## 3.9 Scenic Routes

- **Cote d'Azur coastal run:** Marseille to Nice - rocky headlands, villas, yacht harbours
- **Paris-Nice sleeper:** Mediterranean coast morning views
- **Nice-Digne Railway:** Private scenic line (trainprovence.com)
- **Petit Train Jaune:** Narrow gauge through Pyrenees
- **Corsica railways:** Island rail network

## 3.10 Quirks & Warnings

- TER "fully booked" displays are often false; buy ticket for alternate time as workaround
- Ouigo connections are unprotected (no rebooking if delayed). Allow 2 hours Paris connection southbound, 2.5 hours northbound
- SNCF photo upload for Carte Avantage broken; carry photo ID
- Billet Bambin: EUR 9 for infant (0-3) seat on TGV/Intercites
- Children under 12: child rate. Under 4: free without seat
- Disruptions: check sncf-connect.com/app/en-en/trafficInfo

## 3.11 Unusual Destinations

- **Mont Saint-Michel:** No station, train + bus required
- **St Tropez:** Train to St Raphael, then bus (EUR 3, ~1h25)
- **Corsica:** Ferry from Marseille, Toulon, or Nice (Corsicalinea.com or CorsicaFerries.com)

## 4.6 Reservation Requirements

**ALL long-distance trains require mandatory reservations.** This includes AVE, Alvia, Intercity, Euromed, and even some shorter regional services.

- Cannot simply board; must have seat assigned
- Passholder reservations difficult to arrange from outside Spain
- Cercanias/Rodalies suburban trains: no reservation needed

## 4.7 Security Procedures

**X-ray baggage screening required** before boarding high-speed trains at major stations (Barcelona Sants, Madrid Atocha/Chamartin). Arrive with buffer time.

**Ouigo:** Formal 30-minute check-in applies.

**Practical note:** Security is described as "basically security theatre" with minimal metal detection enforcement, but baggage X-ray is consistent.

**TIP:** Keep penknives, corkscrews etc. in pockets (not baggage) to avoid confiscation by over-zealous security staff during X-ray screening.

## 4.8 Luggage

- Standard baggage included on Renfe (25 kg per bag stated but not enforced)
- Budget operators enforce strict limits:
  - Ouigo: EUR 5 surcharge per suitcase; strict size limits (reported EUR 30 charge for bags "a few millimetres over")
  - Avlo: budget airline-style restrictions
- Iryo Inicial: 1 small bag + 1 cabin-size bag; larger bags in higher classes

## 4.9 Amenities

**All operators provide:**
- Power sockets at all seats
- Free WiFi
- Air conditioning

**Food & Drink:**
- Cafe-bars on long-distance trains: tea, coffee, beer, wine, snacks, microwaved hot dishes
- No catering on some local/regional trains - bring your own
- Renfe Premium: hot tray meal with wine
- Iryo Infinita Bistro: light meal with drinks
- Add meal boxes to Elige Confort fares (12+ hours notice on renfe.com)

## 4.10 Combinado Cercanias (Free Suburban Connections)

Long-distance tickets automatically include **free suburban train travel** within:
- 3 hours before departure
- 4 hours after arrival

Available in: Barcelona, Madrid, Malaga, Valencia, Seville, Bilbao, Zaragoza, and others.
Access via QR code scanner on Cercanias/Rodalies gates.

## 4.11 Discount Cards

### Tarjeta Dorada (Over-60)
- EUR 6 annually
- 25-40% discounts
- **Only purchasable in person at Spanish stations** (useless for advance online booking)

### Children & Infants
- Children under 14: reduced fares
- Infants under 4: free but **mandatory free ticket required**
- "If you forget, ask for one at a Renfe ticket office before boarding"

## 4.12 Interrail/Eurail Pass Usage

- Spain is **"not the most pass-friendly country"**
- Every long-distance train and some regional ones require paid reservation (EUR 7-10)
- Many trains cannot be reserved online; must queue at Spanish stations
- **Recommendation:** Mix-and-match pass with regular tickets for some journeys
- From April 1, 2025: passes valid on Iryo (with reservation fee)
- NOT valid on Ouigo

### Renfe Spain Pass
- 4-10 one-way journeys in one month
- Reservations included, no extra fees
- First reservation activates one-month validity

## 4.13 Station Information

### Barcelona Sants
- Central location with metro and taxi connections
- X-ray baggage screening for high-speed platforms
- Left luggage (consigna) available
- Rodalies/Cercanias ticket office for local trains
- Ticket machines do NOT sell international tickets to Latour de Carol

### Madrid Stations
- **Atocha:** Southern high-speed services
- **Chamartin:** Northern services
- Transfer service available between stations
- System selects appropriate station with "MADRID (TODAS)"
- Sala Club lounges at both for Premium fare holders

## 4.14 Quirks & Warnings

1. Renfe unpredictably delays opening bookings - if data looks incomplete, WAIT
2. Overseas card rejection at renfe.com is common - use thetrainline.com
3. Madrid has TWO main stations requiring transfers
4. Rodalies/Cercanias local trains will NOT appear on normal train booking sites

### Latour de Carol
- Remote Pyrenean border station "in the middle of nowhere, ringed by mountains"

# 6. INTERRAIL/EURAIL PASS STRATEGY

## 6.1 Eurostar Pass Usage

| Class | Passholder Fare |
|-------|----------------|
| Standard (with 2nd class pass) | GBP 35 |
| Plus (with 1st class pass) | GBP 40-43 |

**Requirements:**
- Must hold **Global Pass** (country passes don't qualify)
- Limited places available
- Rule of thumb: if regular fare is GBP 143 or less, passholder places available; at GBP 160+, typically not
- Book at raileurope.com (GBP 2/person fee), b-europe.com (GBP 4 total), or interrail.eu (GBP 2/person)

**Home country rule (UK Interrail holders):** Eurostar counts as home country journey, using one of two allowed home country travel days.

**Tip if unavailable:** Book London-Lille instead, then use French domestic train Lille-Paris.

## 6.2 France Pass Usage

- **TGV:** Mandatory reservation, EUR 10-20 supplement per journey
- **Intercites (some):** Mandatory reservation, EUR 10-20
- **TER regional:** No reservation, no supplement - free with pass
- **Ouigo:** NOT available for pass holders
- For one or two specific journeys, regular advance tickets may be cheaper than pass + supplements

## 6.3 Spain Pass Usage

- **Not pass-friendly:** Every long-distance train requires paid reservation (EUR 7-10)
- Many trains cannot be reserved online from outside Spain
- Must queue at Spanish station ticket offices
- **Recommendation:** Mix pass with regular tickets
- From April 2025: passes also valid on Iryo
- NOT valid on Ouigo Spain

## 6.4 When a Pass Makes Sense

**Pass is worth it when:**
- Traveling across multiple countries
- Need flexibility (don't want train-specific tickets)
- Fares are expensive (peak season/late booking)
- Long journey from home station requiring coverage

**Pass is NOT worth it when:**
- Making just 1-2 specific journeys (advance tickets cheaper)
- Traveling primarily in Spain (reservation hassle)
- Only doing London-Paris return (regular advance tickets cheaper)

## 6.5 Renfe Spain Pass (Alternative)
- 4-10 one-way journeys in one month
- Reservations INCLUDED (no extra fees)
- First reservation activates one-month validity
- Better option than Interrail for Spain-only travel
